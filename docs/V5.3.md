## Evidence

Reference: user's 1919×1079 Chrome split-screen screenshot from 2026-11-22. The older notGoogle pane had approximately a 730px search bar versus 690px in Google, and a 233px logo glyph versus 269px. Background samples from the screenshot were `#202124` vs `#22242a`. V5.1 already corrected the page background and search-surface colors; V5.3 preserves those corrections rather than claiming to rediscover them.

These are screenshot estimates; the original browser zoom/device DPR were not captured. They are **not** a proof of 1:1 visual equivalence or evidence of Google's source CSS.
